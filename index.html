UTS on Mobile Ticketing Guide & Geofencing Rules

Indian Railways official guidelines for paperless ticketing, GPS boundaries, and season pass renewal.

CR Official Commuter Advisory
1

GPS Geofencing Inner & Outer Limits

  • Outer Range: You can book paperless tickets from up to 20 km away from the boarding station (home or office).
  • Inner Safety Zone: Booking is blocked within 20-30 meters of railway tracks and platforms to stop hazardous last-minute boarding.
  • Always book your ticket before reaching the station foot overbridge.
2

Paperless vs Paper Ticket Mode

  • Paperless Mode: Stored on phone with animated security hologram. Screenshots are strictly invalid for checking. Ensure device has battery.
  • Paper Mode: If GPS is disabled or you prefer physical tickets, select Paper mode and print ticket at ATVM / CoTVM kiosks with the booking ID.
3

Railway R-Wallet 3% Instant Cashback

  • Recharging your UTS Railway Wallet (R-Wallet) gives an instant 3% bonus credit.
  • Recharge ₹1,000 to get ₹1,030 balance immediately. Zero payment gateway convenience fees.
  • Recharge via UPI, Debit Card, or NetBanking within the UTS app.
4

Advance Suburban Pass Renewal (10 Days)

  • You can renew your Monthly or Quarterly Suburban Season Pass up to 10 days in advance before current pass expiry.
  • New validity starts automatically from the day after old pass expires. Avoids month-end booking queues.
  • Valid for all classes: II Class, I Class, and AC Suburban.

Pro Tip: End coaches (Coach 1 and Coach 12) typically have less crowd density compared to central coaches (Coach 5-7) which align directly with major station foot-over-bridges (FOBs).

🎒 Front Bag Discipline

Always turn backpacks around to your chest before entering the coach. This prevents snagging doors, protects valuables, and gives you breathing space.

🚪 Center Pole Etiquette

Never cling to the center doorway pole if you are traveling more than 2 halts. Move inward to the aisle ("Pudhe chala") to let alightees exit smoothly.

❄️ AC Local Dwell Time

AC EMU automated doors close strictly 15-20 seconds before departure. Never obstruct door closing tracks as sensors will lock brakes and trigger alarms.

Monsoon & Flood Transit Guide (Rain Saathi)

Central Railway waterlogging flashpoints, track submersion thresholds, pumping operations & storm rules.

Monsoon Readiness Manual

⛈️ Critical Low-Lying Track Sections (High Waterlogging Risk)

Sion – Kurla Dip (Km 13-15) Mithi River Basin

Tracks dip 1.2m below road grade adjacent to the Mithi River. High tides above 4.5m coupled with >50mm/hr rain cause water ingress. 24 high-capacity pumps stationed.

Chunabhatti & Tilak Nagar Harbour / Chord

Culvert bottle-necks near Kurla car shed. Trains operate under slow caution (15 km/h) if water touches rail base.

Vidyavihar & Kanjurmarg Dips Slow Line Tracks

Fast corridor remains elevated; Slow corridor tracks experience slow water drainage during intense cloudbursts.

Kalwa – Mumbra Creek Alignment Tidal Backwater

Thane Creek tidal surges can slow speed of local rakes. Anti-flood micro-tunnels and boulder bunds protect rails.

📏 Railway Water-Level Safety Thresholds (Safety Operating Protocol)

Indian Railways Operating Code
Under 75 mm
Normal Operation

Water below rail head; services run at regular timetable speeds.

75 mm – 150 mm
Caution Speed (15 km/h)

Motorman pilots at crawling speed to prevent water splash into undercarriage traction motors.

Above 150 mm
Power Cut & Suspension

Overhead 25kV traction automatically switched off to prevent short circuits and electrocution hazards.

✅ Recommended Monsoon Commuter Actions
  • Check CentralSaathi delay telemetry before leaving home during red alerts.
  • Stay inside the rake if a train halts between stations. The coaches offer safe insulation against 25kV traction.
  • Listen attentively to the Motorman/Guard public address announcements for track clearance updates.
⛔ Strictly Prohibited Monsoon Hazards
  • Never jump onto waterlogged tracks: High risk of hidden open manholes, muck slips, and 3rd-rail electric leaks.
  • Do not pull the emergency alarm chain: Pulling the chain freezes brakes and traps following trains in flooded sectors.
  • Do not touch metallic track signaling posts or overhead wire mast foundations in deep water.

Passenger Charter & Commuter Rights

Know your legal rights during mega blocks, AC local cancellations, delay refunds, and accidental coverage.

Citizen Charter

🔄 Mega Block Travel Permissibility

When Central Railway announces major traffic/power blocks or waterlogging disruptions, valid suburban season ticket holders are legally permitted to travel via alternative suburban routes (e.g. via Harbour line or Trans-Harbour line) without paying extra fare.

❄️ AC Local Substitution & Refund

If a scheduled AC suburban service is replaced by a regular non-AC local rake or cancelled, AC pass holders can travel in First Class coaches. Single ticket holders can claim a 100% fare refund at station booking counters within 3 hours.

🎟️ Statutory Concessions

  • Students: 50% discount on suburban season tickets (Free passes for girls up to Std 12).
  • Divyangjan / Patients: Up to 75% concession with verified railway certificate.
  • Senior Citizens: Reserved priority seating in all II & I class compartments.

🏥 Golden Hour Accident Treatment

Railways are bound by Supreme Court guidelines to provide immediate free first aid at Station Master Emergency Medical Rooms (One Rupee Clinics) and arrange instant free ambulance transit (Dial 108) to the nearest municipal hospital.
